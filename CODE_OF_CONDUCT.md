# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Voidstrap should be a welcoming place to ask questions, report problems, and contribute. Everyone deserves respectful treatment, regardless of age, background, identity, ability, or experience.

By participating in the repository or the Voidstrap Discord server, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

## Expected behavior

* Be respectful and patient, especially when someone is learning or reporting a problem for the first time.
* Give specific, constructive feedback about the work. Explain disagreements without attacking the person.
* Accept corrections in good faith, and take responsibility when your behavior causes harm.
* Keep discussions relevant to the issue or pull request. Allow maintainers time to respond.
* Respect other people's privacy, boundaries, and right to disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
* Hateful content, sexual material, graphic violence, or targeted slurs.
* Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, identity, login cookie, or account details, without permission.
* Promoting cheating, exploitation, account theft, or scams.
* Spam, unsolicited advertising, repeated demands for attention, or deliberate disruption of a discussion.
* Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith, or encouraging others to break these rules.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Voidstrap's GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and Discord server. It also applies when someone represents the project in another community or public space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately:

* **Discord:** send a direct message to a Voidstrap staff member on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/5tJBqBH8ck).
* **GitHub:** use **Report content** in the item's menu to report a violation to GitHub. This is GitHub's reporting process; it does not replace contacting Voidstrap staff about project moderation.

Include what happened, where it happened, and when. Links or screenshots can help, but do not publish sensitive information or confront the person publicly to gather evidence.

Reports are handled privately and shared only as needed to investigate and respond. People who report concerns in good faith must not face retaliation. If a report involves a staff member, contact another staff member.

For a software vulnerability, follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

## Enforcement

Voidstrap maintainers and Discord staff review reports and choose a response based on the behavior, its impact, and any previous incidents. Responses may include:

1. A request to stop the behavior or correct a contribution.
2. A private warning that explains the problem and expected change.
3. A temporary restriction or ban.
4. A permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers may also remove, edit, or reject comments, code, issues, and other contributions that violate these rules. Serious incidents may require immediate action without an earlier warning.

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, contact staff privately with the relevant context. Do not use unrelated issues or pull requests to dispute it.
